Program Details

How the New Orleans E-Bike Incentive Program Works

The City of New Orleans is offering 3,000 e-bike rebates to residents — funded 100% by a federal EPA Climate Pollution Reduction Grant. Here's what you need to know:

$1,200
Income-qualified residents
(2,000 rebates reserved)
$600
All other eligible
New Orleans residents
  • 📋 3,000 total rebates — 2,000 are reserved for income-qualified residents. The remaining 1,000 are available to all eligible New Orleans residents.
  • 🔒 UL 2849 certification required — the bike you buy must carry this safety certification. Every bike on our floor does.
  • 🏪 Redeemed in person, at the shop — this isn't a mail-in rebate. You bring your voucher to JBird Bikes and apply it right at checkout.
  • 🚫 Online retailers cannot participate — the program is limited to local retailers that service and repair what they sell. Amazon, online-only shops, and out-of-city vendors are not eligible.
  • 💚 100% federally funded — no city budget dollars. The program is backed by the EPA Climate Pollution Reduction Grant.
All program details, eligibility rules, and application information come from the official City of New Orleans E-Bike Incentive Program page. Check there for current availability and requirements.

Every Bike is UL 2849 Certified

UL 2849 is an independent electrical safety standard for e-bikes — it means the battery and motor have been tested and certified as safe. Every bike on our floor carries this certification, so every bike qualifies for the rebate. No guessing, no exceptions.
