Privacy & Terms

1. Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Mobile SMS Messaging

At JBird Bikes, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when opting into our mobile SMS messaging services.

Information Collection & Consent
We collect your mobile phone number only when you explicitly provide it to us and opt-in to receive text messages (e.g., via our website sign-up forms or during a past booking process). By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive promotional and informational text messages regarding our local e-bike initiative, sales, and updates.

How We Use Your Information
Your mobile number is used exclusively to deliver the text message updates you requested. We do not use your information for any other undisclosed purposes.

Sharing of Information (Strict Carrier Compliance)
No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt-Out & Help
If you wish to stop receiving text messages from us, you can reply STOP to any message you receive. To get help, reply HELP or contact us directly at info@originebikes.com or (504) 521-6997.

2. Terms and Conditions

Terms and Conditions for Mobile SMS Messaging

By opting into the mobile SMS messaging service provided by JBird Bikes, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

Program Description
Subscribers will receive promotional and informational text messages regarding our local e-bike initiative, special offers, and store updates from JBird Bikes via short code or toll-free/local numbers.

Message Frequency & Rates
Message frequency varies based on user interaction and ongoing campaigns, but will typically not exceed 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Please contact your wireless provider for details on your text plan.

Supported Carriers
Our messaging service is available across all major United States mobile carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to Opt-Out / Stop
You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text STOP to our number. After you send the SMS message STOP to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.

How to Get Help
If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program you can reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or you can get help directly by contacting (504) 521-6997.
