1. Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Mobile SMS Messaging

At JBird Bikes, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when opting into our mobile SMS messaging services.

Information Collection & Consent

We collect your mobile phone number only when you explicitly provide it to us and opt-in to receive text messages (e.g., via our website sign-up forms or during a past booking process). By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive promotional and informational text messages regarding our local e-bike initiative, sales, and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How We Use Your Information

Your mobile number is used exclusively to deliver the text message updates you requested. We do not use your information for any other undisclosed purposes.

Sharing of Information (Strict Carrier Compliance)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt-Out & Help

If you wish to stop receiving text messages from us, you can reply STOP to any message you receive. To get help, reply HELP or contact us directly at info@originebikes.com or (504) 521-6997.
